Twenty cents per million input tokens changes what is worth automating. At this level the cost of a call stops being the constraint on design and volume becomes the question instead. Fast mode being priced separately makes latency an explicit purchase rather than a property of the model.

INDUSTRY

Seniority Has Stopped Predicting Operating Ability

  • Turing Post quoted the argument that it is a bull market for AI-native individual contributors and a bear market for heads of function.Turing Post
  • The deliberately exaggerated claim is that a year managing ten agents may now teach more than ten years managing teams.Turing Post
  • Turing Post called it half-right: agents teach decomposition, context design, evaluation and exception handling, but not how an institution reacts when automation reaches people's roles.Turing Post
Why it matters

Managing agents and managing people teach different halves of the job. The technical half is now learnable in a year, which compresses one kind of seniority. The half about how organisations absorb change is not, and is exactly what gets tested when the automation lands.

Concepts to know

Pareto frontier
AINews

The set of models that are not beaten on both cost and capability at once. Moving it means offering something no existing model matches on both axes.

Post-training update
AINews

Improving a model without pretraining it again: cheaper, faster, and a signal that method rather than scale produced the gain.

AI-native individual contributor
Turing Post

Someone who works directly with agents rather than through a team. The hiring signal Turing Post describes favours them over managers whose experience is entirely with people.
