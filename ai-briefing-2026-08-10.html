Why it matters

Employment statistics arrive months after the decisions they describe. Hiring freezes and unbackfilled roles do not appear as job losses, so the effect shows up as an absence of openings rather than as a number. That is invisible to the series most policy responds to.

Concepts to know

Critical capability rating
The Neuron

A written threshold at which a lab restricts or delays a model. OpenAI applying one to Astra makes the decision auditable rather than a matter of judgement.

Data centre revolt
The Neuron

Organised local opposition to AI infrastructure. It does not stop the buildout but decides where it happens, which drives both cost and schedule.

Unbackfilled role
Brain Buzz

A job left empty after someone leaves rather than cut. It does not appear in layoff figures, which is why employment data lags what is happening.
