GPT-5.6 Sol Scores 7.8 Percent on ARC-AGI-3

  • GPT-5.6 Sol scores just 7.8 percent on ARC-AGI-3 despite having solved longstanding open problems in mathematics and beaten games.TLDR AI
  • A separate write-up described enabling two settings that tripled scores on the same benchmark.TLDR AI
Why it matters

The gap between solving research problems and scoring on ARC is the whole point of ARC. A model can be superhuman on hard specific problems and weak on the general abstraction the benchmark tests. That two configuration settings triple the score says as much about benchmark sensitivity as about capability.

Agents Are Reviving Ontologies as Logical Guardrails

  • A widely watched AI Engineer World's Fair talk argued that agentic systems need logical guardrails, by which the speaker meant ontologies.AINews
  • An ontology describes the classes, properties and relationships in a domain, or as the speaker put it, data as graphs.AINews
  • The argument is that LLMs supply probabilistic reasoning and need something structured underneath it.AINews
Why it matters

The semantic web's idea is returning as scaffolding for agents. An agent that can act needs to be right about what things are, not merely plausible. A structured model of the domain constrains what a probabilistic system is allowed to conclude.

Airbnb Reviews a Hundred Outputs Before Writing Any Automated Eval

  • Airbnb evaluates generative AI by reviewing 100 prototype outputs to find real failure modes before creating automated evals.TLDR AI
  • It then uses programmatic checks against the failures it actually found.TLDR AI
Why it matters

Write the test after you know how it breaks, not before. Automated evals written in advance measure the failures somebody imagined. A hundred real outputs cost a day and tell you which ones actually happen.

Concepts to know

Ontology
AINews

A description of the classes, properties and relationships in a domain: data as graphs. Proposed as the structure that constrains what an agent may conclude, rather than leaving it to probability alone.

Post-training
The Deep View

The work done on a model after pretraining to make it useful and safe. It is skilled research work, which is why a model doing it for another model is significant.

ARC-AGI-3
TLDR AI

A benchmark testing abstract reasoning on problems that resist memorisation. Frontier models score in single digits while solving far harder-sounding tasks elsewhere.
