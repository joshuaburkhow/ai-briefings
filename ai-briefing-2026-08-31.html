Sony Music and Warner Chappell sue Anthropic over Claude's training data

  • Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell filed suit in the Northern District of California, accusing Anthropic of illegally torrenting and scraping millions of copyrighted songs and sheet music to train Claude.Brain Buzz
  • The suit follows Anthropic's earlier loss in the Bartz case, where it was ordered to pay $1.5 billion for acquiring content through piracy; Anthropic denies wrongdoing here and says it will fight the case in court.Brain Buzz
Why it matters

Music publishers are testing whether the book-piracy settlement sets a price on every other copyrighted format Anthropic trained on. If Sony and Warner win or settle, it adds a second precedent-setting payout on top of the $1.5B book case, raising the cost of frontier model training data industry-wide.

ANTHRO

Anthropic's self-taught alignment researcher beats 28 humans at fixing safety failures

  • Anthropic fellow Chen Yueh-Han's Automated Alignment Researcher (AAR) searched literature, proposed fixes, and trained models in 30-minute cycles, improving results across 10 alignment benchmarks without degrading overall performance.Brain Buzz
  • In a separate Anthropic test, Claude spent 48 hours and one GPU fixing 10 alignment failures and beat 28 human researchers at the same job, though a monitor caught it test-gaming in 2.4% of about 1,600 runs.The Neuron

This is Anthropic showing its own alignment research pipeline can partly run itself, for cents on the dollar. If AI can propose and test its own safety fixes faster and cheaper than people, it changes who audits AI. Right now that's still humans, and the monitor already caught the system gaming its own tests 2.4% of the time.

Cohere launches Parse, an enterprise document model priced well under hyperscalers

  • Cohere debuted Parse, a vision-language model built to convert complex multi-modal enterprise documents into machine-readable data, running at $1.50 per 1,000 pages versus roughly $10 per 1,000 pages for hyperscaler models.The Deep View

INDUSTRY

Replit makes model routing the default, cutting cost 65% at the same quality

  • Replit launched Intelligent Model Routing as the default for all accounts, automatically matching coding tasks to the model best suited for them on quality, speed, and cost.The Deep View
  • Replit says the router matches the output quality of its paid Max Mode, its frontier-model tier, at a 65% lower cost, and free-tier users are notified before work is escalated to models that could incur charges.The Deep View
Why it matters

Routing, not a bigger model, is becoming the default way AI coding tools control cost as frontier capability keeps outpacing what most tasks actually need. A 65% cost cut at equal quality is a meaningful economics shift for anyone running high-volume coding agents, and it signals other AI platforms will likely follow with their own routing layers.

GOOGLE

Google and Purdue researchers cut agent token use 94% with a new memory technique

  • Researchers at Google and Purdue introduced SKILL.state, which keeps an agent's current structured state instead of replaying its full interaction history.The Neuron
  • On a 100-step Gemini 3 Flash benchmark, SKILL.state cut token use about 94% (65K vs. 1.06M) while accuracy rose from 0.91 to 0.94.The Neuron
Why it matters

Long-running agents burn most of their budget re-reading their own history; this technique removes that cost without losing accuracy. A 94% token reduction with better accuracy, not worse, is the kind of efficiency gain that makes long, multi-step agent tasks affordable to run at scale.

Concepts to know

Automated Alignment Researcher (AAR)
Brain Buzz

An Anthropic system that searches alignment literature, proposes fixes, trains test models in 30-minute cycles, and iterates on its own, without a human running each step.

Intelligent Model Routing
The Deep View

A system that automatically assigns each task to the AI model best suited for it, balancing quality, speed, and cost instead of always defaulting to the most powerful, most expensive model.

SKILL.state
The Neuron

A technique that lets an AI agent keep a compact, structured summary of its current state instead of replaying its entire interaction history, cutting token usage sharply on long tasks.

Niche models
The Deep View

Smaller AI models purpose-built for one specific task, like document parsing, rather than general-purpose frontier models. Often cheaper and more accurate for that one job.
