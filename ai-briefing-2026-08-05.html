AI Collapses the Time From Disclosure to Exploit

  • Frontier models are collapsing the time between vulnerability disclosure and exploit creation from weeks to hours, while producing a flood of new vulnerabilities.TLDR AI
  • Manual triage and traditional patch cycles cannot keep up.TLDR AI
Why it matters

Patch windows were sized for human attackers. If exploitation takes hours, a monthly patch cycle is not slow, it is irrelevant. The response has to be prioritisation by exploitability rather than by severity score, because the queue no longer drains.
