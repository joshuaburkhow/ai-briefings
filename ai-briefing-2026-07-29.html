Concepts to know

Open weights
TLDR AI

Releasing a model's trained parameters so anyone can run and adapt it. Distinct from open source, which would also mean the training code and data. The argument is about which capability levels should be releasable rather than whether any should.

Distillation
TLDR AI

Training a smaller model on a larger one's outputs to copy its behaviour cheaply. Anthropic named industrial-scale distillation as a control point, because it moves capability without moving weights.

Financial backstop
TLDR AI

A guarantee by one party to cover another's borrowing. Nvidia's proposed role in the Ohio project would let OpenAI raise money it could not raise on its own credit.
