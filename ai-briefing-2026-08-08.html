Concepts to know

Cost per test
AINews

Benchmark cost measured per completed task rather than per token. It is the figure that matches what an agent actually costs, since one task may take many calls.

Custom ASIC
AINews

A chip designed for one workload. It only pays back at high, stable volume, which is why several companies committing at once says something about how predictable inference has become.

Reasoning depth control
AI Breakfast

Letting the caller choose how much thinking a model does. OpenAI shipped it as a Think button, turning reasoning into something bought per request.
