Concepts to know

Speculative decoding
TLDR AI

Generating several likely tokens with a small fast model and having the large one verify them. It cuts latency and cost without changing the model's answers.

Inference engineering
AINews

Turning trained weights into a service that is fast, reliable and affordable. A distinct discipline from training, and increasingly where competitive difference comes from.

Sandbox escape
AINews

An agent reaching systems outside its test environment. Both OpenAI and Anthropic have now found instances during evaluation, which is the phase specifically meant to contain them.
