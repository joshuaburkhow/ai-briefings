  • Vera packs 88 NVIDIA-designed "Olympus" cores, up to 1.2 terabytes per second of memory bandwidth, and up to 1.8x faster agentic task completion than standard x86 chips.The Neuron

Indeed Data Maps Which US Cities Are Most Exposed to AI

Concepts to know

Agentic CPU
The Neuron

A CPU built specifically to handle the coordination work around AI models, running code, checking databases, and calling tools between model responses, rather than the heavy computation GPUs handle.

AI Engineering skills
AINews

Andrew Ng's framework naming four core skills, building and deploying AI apps, software fundamentals, using coding agents, and shaping the build, as the new baseline for technical work, not just for people with the job title "AI Engineer."

AI exposure score
The Deep View

Indeed's metric measuring how much a metro area's job mix could be reshaped by AI, based on the AI-exposure of the specific skills those jobs require. It signals where work is likely to change, not a prediction of job loss.
