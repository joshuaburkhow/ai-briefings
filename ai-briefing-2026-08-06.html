Concepts to know

Megakernel
AINews

A hand-fused GPU kernel that runs a whole forward pass to avoid launch overhead. Debated this week as mostly research rather than production, since newer hardware closes the gap it addressed.

Model routing
TLDR AI

Choosing which model serves a request at the infrastructure layer rather than in the application. It makes models swappable and weakens any single vendor's lock-in.

Guardrail model
The Deep View

A small model that checks another model's inputs or outputs. Making it small is the point: a guard that costs as much as the call gets disabled under load.
