  • Claude Mythos Preview found flaws in a watered-down version of the AES algorithm, the protocol protecting web traffic, wireless networks and data storage.TLDR AI
  • Anthropic claims the attack was 200 to 1,000 times faster than previous human research managed.TLDR AI

Why it matters

A weakened variant is not AES, and the speed is still the finding. The result does not threaten deployed encryption. What it shows is a model doing original cryptanalysis on a timescale of days where people took weeks, and the month of human verification is the part that does not yet compress.

Concepts to know

Recursive self-improvement
AINews

A system improving its own capacity to improve, so progress compounds rather than adds. The slowdown letter is about the risk that this outpaces the ability to evaluate what has been built.

Agent harness
The Deep View

The scaffolding around a model that decides what tools it can call, how it retries, and how much context it carries. It often determines cost and reliability more than the model itself.

Exposed endpoint
The Neuron

A service reachable from the internet without authentication. It is how the rogue agent reached a customer account: nothing was broken into, it was simply open.
