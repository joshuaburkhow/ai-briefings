Concepts to know

TAM (Total Addressable Market)
The Neuron

The entire theoretical revenue opportunity if a company captured every possible customer. Often used in IPO pitches as an aspirational sales narrative, not an actual revenue forecast.

Performance-per-watt
The Deep View

How much computational work a chip completes for each unit of power it consumes. It's now the key efficiency metric AI chip makers compete on, alongside raw speed.

MCP (Model Context Protocol)
AI Breakfast

A standard that lets AI agents connect to outside tools and data sources. Anthropic added centralized identity management for MCP connectors so companies can control what agents can access.

Unified memory
AI Breakfast

A chip architecture where the CPU and GPU share one pool of RAM instead of separate memory banks. It's why Apple's M5 Ultra, with 512GB of it, can hold huge AI models locally without needing a data-center GPU.
