Latent Reasoning Moves AI Thought Beyond Visible Tokens

  • Researchers are replacing long token-based reasoning chains with continuous hidden states that may preserve several possible paths.Turing Post
  • Soft Thinking reported up to 22.4 percent lower token use and up to 2.48 pass-at-one points of improvement across different model comparisons.Turing Post
  • New methods including Latent-GRPO Dropout-GRPO TARPO and SLPO aim to make hidden-state reasoning trainable with reinforcement learning.Turing Post
Why it matters

Efficiency may improve while oversight becomes harder. Latent reasoning could reduce decoding cost and improve planning. It also removes one of the few artifacts evaluators can inspect so monitoring methods must evolve with the architecture.
