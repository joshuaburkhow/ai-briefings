OPENAI

OpenAI gates Astra after it crosses the 'Critical' cyber threshold and finds two zero-days solo

  • Astra became the first OpenAI model to cross the Critical threshold on the Preparedness Framework, scoring 100% on ExploitBench and independently chaining two zero-day exploits in V8 without human help.AI Breakfast
  • The jump comes from a 'recurrent depth' architecture that loops inputs through model layers multiple times, boosting performance but making the model's reasoning harder for safety monitors to trace.AI Breakfast
  • OpenAI is limiting Astra's cyber toolkit to select partners in its Daybreak defense coalition while adding chain-of-thought monitoring and stricter prompt limits, following last month's Hugging Face environment breach by an unreleased model.TLDR AI

Why it matters

The first AI model has crossed the line into finding real security holes entirely on its own. Astra didn't just pass a cyber benchmark, it found two real zero-day bugs and chained them into working exploits without a human walking it through the steps. OpenAI locking it down to a small partner coalition shows even the model's own maker isn't ready to hand that capability out broadly.

Concepts to know

Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS)
AI Breakfast

A new Anthropic feature that lets business customers keep AI monitoring data and encryption keys inside their own cloud infrastructure instead of Anthropic's, similar in effect to a zero-data-retention policy.

Recurrent depth
AI Breakfast

A model design that loops the same input through its layers multiple times instead of running once straight through. It boosts capability and cuts compute cost, but makes the model's internal reasoning harder for safety monitors to trace.

Hybrid Compute
The Deep View

Perplexity's system that automatically decides, task by task, whether to run AI processing locally on your device or send it to a cloud model, keeping sensitive data off external servers.

AIDR (AI Detection and Response)
The Deep View

A new security product category CrowdStrike is creating with Falcon Guardian: software that finds every AI agent running on a company's network, tracks what each one does, and blocks agents that go rogue.
