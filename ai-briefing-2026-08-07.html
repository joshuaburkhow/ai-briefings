Concepts to know

Resumability
AINews

The ability to continue a long agent run after a failure rather than restarting. Meta's local event log is what provides it, and it is what makes multi-day autonomy practical.

Self-improving harness
AINews

Scaffolding that modifies its own strategy between attempts. Prime Agent's ARC claim comes from the harness rather than a new model, which is why the number moved so far so fast.
