Transactional Assistants Need Better Success Metrics

  • Useful evaluation should separate intent recognition factual accuracy completion rate option quality and user control.Turing Post
  • Accuracy scores can hide differences in answer coverage abstention retries and time to completion.Turing Post
  • A platform may complete more purchases because it controls checkout rather than because it recommended the best option.Turing Post
Why it matters
