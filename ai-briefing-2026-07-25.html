Concepts to know

Open-weight model
Everyday AI

A model whose trained parameters are available for others to download inspect or run even when the full training data and code are not open.

Model router
TLDR AI

A service that gives developers one interface and chooses or exposes different AI models based on capability cost or preference.

Recursive improvement
Hugging Face Daily Papers

A process in which an agent evaluates and revises its own research method across repeated cycles instead of following one fixed plan.
