EU classifies ChatGPT as a 'Very Large Platform,' triggering stricter DSA rules

  • The EU now classifies ChatGPT, alongside Reddit and Roblox, as a 'Very Large Platform' under the Digital Services Act because it exceeds 45 million monthly EU users.Brain Buzz
  • The designation requires OpenAI to stop targeting ads at minors, avoid using sensitive data like religion or politics, and explain how its recommendation systems work, with compliance due by December 2026.Brain Buzz

This is a separate, additional EU rulebook hitting ChatGPT, on top of the EU AI Act's enforcement phase that started this week. Companies operating ChatGPT-based products in Europe now face two overlapping compliance regimes; the December 2026 deadline is a concrete date to track for any team relying on ChatGPT in EU-facing products.

OpenClaw 2.0 turns the DIY personal AI agent into an out-of-the-box platform

  • The open-source personal-agent project shipped v2026.8.1 ('OpenClaw 2.0'), its largest update yet, with 933 contributors merging more than 16,000 pull requests; setup now reuses existing ChatGPT or Claude subscriptions, API keys, or local models instead of requiring manual terminal setup.The Neuron
  • The release adds Memory for conversation recall and reusable-skill learning, plus experimental 'Swarm' mode (one task spawning parallel subagents) and 'Fleet' mode (multiple isolated agent instances with separate credentials and state).The Neuron
Why it matters

OpenClaw is trying to move personal AI agents from a hobbyist project requiring SSH and API-key plumbing into software normal people can run themselves. It's model-agnostic, running on OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or local models, so it's a preview of what an open agent-orchestration layer looks like once the underlying model choice stops mattering as much as the workflow layer around it.
