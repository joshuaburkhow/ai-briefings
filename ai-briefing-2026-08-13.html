INDUSTRY

Researchers decoded the hidden reasoning frontier labs encrypt, and found real passwords inside

  • A disclosed paper showed the encrypted chain-of-thought blocks that Anthropic, OpenAI and Google hand back to client apps can be replayed into a weaker sibling model from the same provider, which can then be jailbroken into reading the stronger model's hidden reasoning aloud — no encryption key needed, and no attack on the stronger model at all.TLDR AI
  • A scan of roughly 7,000 public traces turned up 62 unique API keys, 33 email addresses and 33 passwords, with 64 items appearing only inside the reasoning blocks and nowhere in the visible session.AINews
  • A wider sweep of 315,320 public reasoning blocks recovered 367 pieces of personal information and 182 credentials; the researchers also saw Kimi K3 producing reasoning strikingly similar to hidden frontier traces, though similarity alone does not prove how it was trained.The Neuron
  • All three labs were notified before publication and several of the vulnerabilities are already fixed, but the recovered token counts matched billed thinking tokens roughly one to one.AI Breakfast
Why it matters

Anything you paste into a public agent session may be carrying secrets you cannot see. The point is not that a lab was careless with a key. It is that hidden reasoning was treated as a black box that could be shipped around between apps, sessions and users, and that box turned out to be readable by a cheaper model from the same family. If you have ever shared a Claude Code or Codex session publicly, the encrypted blob in it is part of what you shared. Treat reasoning traces as data you are publishing, not as opaque plumbing, and stop pasting whole sessions into issue trackers and gists.

OSS

NVIDIA's Switchyard picks a different model for each step of an agent's task

  • NeMo Switchyard is an open-source library that routes each step of an agent workflow to whichever model fits it, rather than committing the whole run to one model.TLDR AI
  • Paired with the new Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, NVIDIA's own tests put task completion at frontier level while costing roughly a third of running Opus 4.8 alone.TLDR AI
  • Lightning itself is a 30-billion-parameter open mixture-of-experts model with about 3 billion active parameters, built for high-volume agent execution and roughly 30% faster than Qwen3.6-35B at matching accuracy.AI Breakfast
Why it matters

The unit of model selection is shrinking from the app to the step. Most teams still pick one model per product and live with it. Switchyard treats that as waste: planning wants a strong model, the eighty tool calls after it do not. If a third of the cost at the same completion rate holds up outside NVIDIA's own benchmarks, per-step routing stops being an optimisation and becomes the default architecture — and the practical question for your own agents becomes which steps genuinely need the expensive model.

Concepts to know

Encrypted reasoning trace
AINews

The private step-by-step thinking a model does before answering, returned to the calling app as a signed, encrypted block so the app can hand it back on the next turn without the app — or a competitor — being able to read it. This week's paper showed the block travels between models, sessions and users, which is what made it attackable.

Distillation
The Neuron

Training a smaller, cheaper model on the outputs of a stronger one to copy some of its capability. Hiding chain-of-thought was the main defence against it, which is why decoded traces matter commercially and not only for privacy.

Mixture of experts (MoE)
TLDR AI

A model split into many specialist sub-networks where only a few activate per token. Nemotron 3.5 Lightning is 30 billion parameters total but around 3 billion active, which is why it serves fast and cheap while still being a large model.

C2PA
The Deep View

An open standard for attaching tamper-evident provenance metadata to a file, recording what created it and what edited it. It is the mechanism behind the AI-labelling commitments now being made under the EU AI Act.
